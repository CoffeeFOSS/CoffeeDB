Username requirements:

  • Between 3 and 20 characters long.
  • Must only contain alphanumeric characters or hyphens (-).

Keep in mind:

  • Changing usernames have a cooldown of one week.
  • Changing the casing of your username (eg. from 'john' to 'joHN') still activates the cooldown.
  • Your old username will be available for other people to claim.
  • You will not be able to login with your old username.
